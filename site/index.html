What you get, and what you do not have to learn

Kafka, run for you

Your own cluster on AWS, not shared with anyone, which we set up, patch and upgrade. Topics come with sensible limits as you create them, and your connection settings come as one file to download. It is plain Apache Kafka: every client library works, and nothing ties you to us.

See what your data is doing

How far behind each consumer is, in minutes rather than offsets. A timeline of what changed, such as a new topic or a rebalance, next to the charts it explains. A map of which applications write to which topics, and who reads them.

No surprise bills

You pay for the data you process, at rates we agree with you up front, and we explain your usage in plain language. Your trial costs nothing, and ends on its own unless you choose to stay.

Answers, not dashboards to decode

Lag in time

"3 minutes behind" says more than "36,141 messages". Changeport measures how old the oldest message is that each consumer group has not read yet, and alerts you, and your webhook, when it falls further behind than you allow.

What changed, and when

When the traffic moves, the timeline shows what happened around it: topics created and changed, consumers joining and leaving, brokers restarting. Who changed what in the console is there too.

Where your data flows

The map draws your producers, the topics they write and the groups that read them, with the traffic on each line and which consumer is behind.

Questions

Is it real Apache Kafka?

Yes. Your applications use the Kafka client libraries they would use anywhere, with a username and password over TLS. There is no Changeport SDK, so you can move your applications to another Kafka at any time.

Where does my cluster run?

On AWS in us-east-1 (Northern Virginia), on servers that run your cluster and no one else's. Production clusters spread their brokers over three availability zones, so the loss of one does not stop them.

Who can see my data?

Your team. The console shows your topics and how they are used, and only your organization's admins can look at messages in it. Our operators run the cluster without reading what you send through it.

Can I get my Postgres tables into Kafka?

Yes. Add your database as a source in the console, run the SQL it gives you, and each table's changes stream into a topic of its own, from the rows that exist today on.

What happens when my trial ends?

If you want to keep your cluster, tell us before the end and it stays as it is. If you do nothing, the cluster and its data are deleted when the trial ends. We email you three days before.

What does it cost?

The trial is free. After it, you pay for the data your cluster processes, at rates we agree with you before you decide to stay.
