Observability

Know how far behind every reader is. In minutes.

Streaming tools count lag in messages: how many a reader has yet to process. Whether 36,000 messages is a problem depends on how fast they arrive. Changeport measures how old the oldest unread message is, and alerts you when it passes your threshold.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Ask in your trial request, and the person who reviews it answers.

Which databases can I stream from?

Postgres today: Amazon RDS and Aurora, Google Cloud SQL, Azure Database, Supabase, Neon, or your own. Add it as a source in the console, run the SQL it gives you, and each table's changes stream into a topic of its own, from the rows that exist today on.

Does it slow my database down?

Changeport reads the write-ahead log, which Postgres keeps anyway, through a replication slot. It never queries your tables for changes. The console shows how much log the slot keeps, so that you see a stalled stream before it fills your disk.

What runs under the hood?

Apache Kafka, run for you on servers of your own. Your applications read and write with the Kafka client libraries they would use anywhere, so there is no Changeport SDK, and nothing ties you to us.

Who can see my data?

Your team. The console shows your streams and how they are used, and only your organization's admins can look at messages in it. Our operators run the service without reading what you send through it.

What happens when my trial ends?

If you want to keep streaming, tell us before the end and everything stays as it is. If you do nothing, your streams and their data are deleted when the trial ends. We email you three days before.

What does it cost?

The trial is free. After it, you pay for the data you stream, at rates we agree with you before you decide to stay.
