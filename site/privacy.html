Privacy policy

What this covers

This policy covers this website and the Changeport console, where your team runs and watches its Kafka cluster. It does not cover the messages you send through your cluster: those are your data, which we process only to run the cluster for you (see the terms).

This website

This website sets no cookies, and uses no analytics, advertising or anything else from another company. Its one script animates the page in your browser, and sends nothing anywhere. Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs, such as your IP address and the page you asked for, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account: your name and email address, which you give when you sign up, so that you can sign in and we can contact you about your account.
  • Your trial request: your company, your role, what you want Kafka for, roughly how much data you expect and when you would like to start, so that we can decide on your request and size your cluster.
  • How you use the console: the changes you make, such as creating a topic, with your name and the time, so that your team can see who changed what, and so that we can investigate problems and keep an audit trail.
  • Your cluster's metrics: how much data flows through it, how far behind each consumer is, and the metrics your Kafka clients report about themselves, so that we can show them to you, alert you and keep your cluster healthy.

Who processes it

  • Clerk runs sign-in and keeps your account.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts your cluster, the console and our email.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, for as long as you have one.
  • A trial request, for as long as we may need it to decide on later requests from your company.
  • Your cluster's metrics and change timeline, for 30 days.
  • When a trial ends or you leave, we delete your cluster at once and keep its data for 30 days in case you return, then delete it for good.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by replying to any email from us. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and tell you by email about anything that matters to you.
