Terms

The service

Changeport runs a dedicated Apache Kafka cluster for your organization on Amazon Web Services, and the console where your team manages and watches it. We set up, patch and upgrade the cluster, and watch it around the clock.

Trials

  • A trial is free and lasts 14 days, unless we agree to extend it.
  • We may accept or decline a trial request, and we tell you why when we decline.
  • We email you three days before your trial ends. If you have not agreed to continue by then, your cluster is deleted at the end of the trial, and its data 30 days later.
  • Trials are for evaluation, not for data you cannot afford to lose.

Your data

The messages you send through your cluster are yours. We process them only to run the cluster for you, and our operators do not read them. You are responsible for having the right to send what you send, and for not sending what the law forbids.

Your account

Keep your credentials and your cluster's password safe; anyone with them can use your cluster. Tell us at once if you think they have been exposed, and rotate them.

Acceptable use

Do not use Changeport to attack or overload other systems, to break the law, or to get around the limits of your cluster. We may suspend a cluster that does, and tell you why.

Fees

After a trial, you pay for the data your cluster processes, at the rates we agree with you in writing before you continue.

Ending

You can end the service at any time by telling us. We delete your cluster when it ends, and its data 30 days later.

Liability

We work to keep your cluster available and your data safe, but we cannot promise the service is never interrupted. Our liability is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before a claim.

Changes

When these terms change, we update this page and tell you by email at least 30 days before the change applies to you.
